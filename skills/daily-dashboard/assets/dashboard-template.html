Welcome! Let’s load your data

This dashboard shows what needs your attention today in your VSCO Workspace: tasks, invoices, quotes, email problems, bookings, leads and your calendar.

  1. Make sure the VSCO connector is connected in your Claude settings.
  2. Press the refresh button (the circular arrow, top right).
  3. When Claude asks, choose Allow so this page can read from your Workspace. It only reads and never changes anything.
  4. Wait up to a minute. Everything fills in from your own account.
Nothing is filtered or hidden: it shows all of your active jobs. Your data stays in your own browser.
